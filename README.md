# Privacy Policy — Momo: Trust Issues

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026
**Developer:** Momo Games (contact: ilyasbabar78@yahoo.com)

## Summary
Momo: Trust Issues is an offline platformer game. The game itself does not
require an account, does not ask for personal information, and does not
collect, store, or share personal data.

## Data the game stores
Game progress (unlocked levels, death count, coins, cosmetic purchases and
settings) is stored **only on your device**. It is never transmitted to us
or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Advertising (upcoming versions)
Future versions of the game may show ads served by **Google AdMob**
(Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process certain device information
(such as advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device data) to serve and
measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Where required, the game will present a consent request for personalized
ads. You can reset or delete your device advertising ID in your device
settings at any time.

## In-app purchases (upcoming versions)
Future versions may offer optional in-app purchases (for example, removing
ads or cosmetic coin packs) processed by Google Play. We do not receive or
store your payment details.

## Children
The game is intended for a general audience (13+). We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes
If our data practices change (for example, when ads are introduced), this
policy will be updated and the effective date revised.
